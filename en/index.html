About Us

Start at Metis, Begin a Global Learning Journey

At Metis Academy, we create a supportive and inspiring learning environment for students from around the world.
We understand the challenges international students face in adjusting to a new language, culture and academic system, so we offer personal guidance every step of the way, from study planning to settling into daily life.
We encourage students to explore and build confidence, helping them become forward-looking thinkers and doers.

Metis is an international academic platform focused on language development, cross-cultural skills and social-emotional support.
Our programs help students build a strong foundation in English, master core subjects, and learn how to succeed in North American universities.
Our graduates leave with solid knowledge, a global perspective and self-motivation, fully prepared for higher education and opportunities around the world.

At Metis Academy, we inspire students to pursue their dreams, embrace diverse cultures, and grow into confident, forward-thinking young leaders.

Jade Wang — Headmaster

Curriculum Overview

Metis Academy offers a well-rounded curriculum that values strong academic foundations as well as cross-cultural awareness and hands-on skills. By combining classroom learning with activities, we help international students adapt to studying abroad and open up new possibilities for their future.



Computer Science

Hands-on courses in up-to-date programming languages and technologies build logical thinking and creativity, laying a solid foundation for future studies in technology and engineering.



Fine Arts

Students express themselves through painting, music and other art forms, developing creativity, strengthening their university applications, and exploring future careers or lifelong interests.



Humanities

Covering language, history, philosophy and social studies, these courses help students understand the world's diverse cultures and sharpen their critical thinking and communication skills.


Science

Through experiments and inquiry, students develop critical thinking, learn to ask bold questions, take on the unknown, and understand the world through the scientific method.


Mathematics

Math is more than numbers and formulas: it trains the mind. Through modelling and real applications, students strengthen their logic and their ability to solve complex problems.


Languages

English improvement and IELTS courses, combined with academic writing and practical communication training, help international students overcome language barriers and fit into an international learning environment.

Co-curricular Activities

Culture & Arts Programs

Learn Peking Opera performance and traditional dough art, experience a blend of Chinese and Western cultures, and gain confidence and creativity through making and performing.

Festival Creative Workshops

Lunar New Year dumplings, Mid-Autumn mooncakes, Christmas gingerbread houses... Make festive treats by hand and share the warmth of the holidays across cultures.

International Summer Camp

Combining art, crafts and performance, the camp makes learning fun while building teamwork and cross-cultural communication skills.

Golf Program

Learn the golf swing and etiquette from professional coaches, building poise, focus and confidence in international social settings.

Flight Program

Step into the cockpit, learn how flight works and try the basic controls, sparking a spirit of exploration and opening the door to future careers in aviation and engineering.
